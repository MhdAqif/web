List in html

Ordered list:

Ordered lists are used for items and display with numbers.

  1. Peter
  2. Harry
  3. Ned

Unordered list:

Unordered lists are used for items in no specific order and display with bullet points.

  • Apple
  • Orange
  • Mango

Description list:

Description list is used to display terms and their descriptions.

Tea
Tea is the second-most consumed beverage in the world, surpassed only by water.
Coffee
Coffee is a beverage brewed from roasted, ground coffee beans.
Boost
Boost is a product that is mixed with the milk.
